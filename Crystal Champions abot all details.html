Crystal Champions

Not yet followed by anyone

Overview
Crystal Champions is a Match 3 game where the player must quickly identify matching colored gems to form connecting patterns to score points. The objective of the game is to score as many points as you can in 3 minutes by arranging gems into lines of three (or more) in a row. Use Special Gems and Void Gems to score bonus points. Clear all levels to have the most time in the highest level multiplier!

Rules
The game begins with an eight-by-eight grid filled with randomly placed gems. Choose two adjacent gems (horizontally or vertically) to switch places on the game board. If the gems swap to create a row of three or more identical gems, a match is made, the gems disappear, and you earn points!

The remaining gems fall down to fill in the empty spots, which can result in additional groups being formed and removed. New gems always fall in from the top of the screen so that the playing area is filled with 64 gems at all times.

 

Score as many points as you can by arranging gems into lines of three (or more) in a row. Use Special Gems and Void Gems to score bonus points.

A match occurs when three or more gems of the same color line up in a row either horizontally or vertically. Matches cannot be made diagonally, and moves that do not result in the creation of a match are not allowed.

If you make a row of four matching gems, one of the pieces will remain on the board as a Fire Gem, which is a type of Special Gem. Use the Fire Gem in a match and watch it blow up all the gems around it. You can also create another type of Special Gem, a Starlight Gem, by making two conjoined matches of 3 ('L' or 'T' shaped). Make a match using the Starlight Gem to destroy all the gems in its row and column. You can combine Special Gems, too! When a Special Gem destroys another Special Gem, the second Special Gem will detonate.

If you create a row of five like-colored gems, one of the pieces will remain and become a Void Gem. When you swap a Void Gem with another gem, ALL gems of the same color will be removed! For example, swap a Void Gem with a red gem and ALL red gems will be removed.

If you ever reach a position in which there are no possible moves, the playing area will be refilled with new gems, and play will continue. There is no scoring penalty for this, but it does cost you time.

After scoring a certain number of points, you will advance to the next level. Reach the following score thresholds to level up through all six levels:

When you level up, the grid will be cleared and filled with new gems. Any unused Special Gems and Void Gems will be carried over to the next level.

You begin each game with three minutes to play. A timer at the bottom of the screen indicates how much time is remaining. When time is up, the game ends.

Click or tap the Hint button at any time to highlight a gem that can be swapped. A hint will also appear automatically after 15 seconds of inaction. In either case, you are penalized 50 points per hint.

Chaining and Live Swaps

Chaining: What happens when continuous matches are made in quick succession. 
Live Swaps: You can swap different gems while others are moving to extend chaining. So don’t wait for the board to fully settle to keep making matches!

  • 20 points for each normal Gem
  • 60 points for each Special Gem destroyed

Special Gems
Each tier of Special Gems creates more deadly detonations as well as unique ways to destroy tricky Gold Panels. Each tier gets increasingly more powerful than the next which will clear more Gems on the board in their detonations.

  • Tier 1: Fire Gem
  • Tier 2: Starlight Gem
  • Tier 3: Void Gem

Match Patterns & Detonations

Match different patterns to make Special Gems.

Fire Gem: Create a line of 4 pattern to make a Fire Gem

Fire Gem detonation = remove 3 x 3 grid around the Fire Gem

Try swapping a Fire Gem near another Fire Gem to create a volley of detonations! Clearing a big space on the board helps create more possibilities of longer chaining.

Starlight Gem: Remove a full row and column around a Starlight Gem

Starlight Gems can be made 2 ways; a match of a 5 in a T or L shape.

Create a match with a Starlight Gem to detonate other special gems in the same line further away from the detonation.

Match 5 in a line to make a Void Gem.

Void Gem: Remove every colored gem that the Void Gem is swapped. Can be swapped with any gem.

Void Gem x Void Gem Swap will clear the entire board! Use this to clear levels faster for many exp points.

Tips

  • Try to level up as quickly as you can. The scoring multipliers for the higher levels can make a huge difference in your score.
  • Look for opportunities to create multiple matches with a single swap. This can earn you tons of extra points if you manage to create a long sequence of matches.
  • Try to detonate several Special Gems at once to take advantage of the bonus for chained explosions.
  • Void Gems are great to use when you are having trouble finding matches. Clearing all gems of one color usually creates a lot of new matches in addition to scoring points.
  • You can make swaps while other pieces are falling! To maximize your score, try to make swaps while your previous matches are still resolving.
  • Use the Hint Button to immediately locate a match to make. It will subtract 50 points from your score, but this can save time to be making more matches.
  • You only have three minutes to score as many points as possible, so think fast and play as quickly as you can!

PowerUp (Game Mode)

Objective
The objective of the game is to clear all Gold Frames on the board by making matches or detonating Special Gems around them, and leveling up. Get to the final level as fast as possible to maximize the highest point multiplier.

Gameplay Elements Key (From Left to Right)

  • Game Timer: Current in-game time remaining
  • Point Multiplier: Current total point multiplier
  • Level: Current level
  • Score: Total player score

Settings Button: Pulls up the settings window

Gold Frame Counter: Current gold frames remaining

Hyperspeed: In-between level complete state. Shows timed duration and increased point multiplier

Gem Tracker: Current colored gems active on the board (Silhouetted when removed from board)

PowerUp Meter (inactive): Current fill progress

PowerUp Meter (active): Upgrade powerup ready

Gold Frames
Gold Frames spawn behind the gems every level. Each level increases the amount of frames spawned. Match or detonate Special Gems close to Gold Frames to remove them. Removing all Gold Frames on the board completes the level and sends you into Hyperspeed. When Hyperspeed ends, you’ll level up and a new set of Gold Frames will appear.

Special Gems
Each tier of Special Gems creates more deadly detonations as well as unique ways to destroy tricky Gold Frames. Each tier gets increasingly more powerful than the next which will clear more Gems on the board in their detonations.

  • Tier 1: Fire Gem
  • Tier 2: Rocket Gem
  • Tier 3: Starlight Gem
  • Tier 4: Void Gem

Match Patterns & Detonations
Match different patterns to make Special Gems.

Fire Gem: create a line of 4 pattern to make a Fire Gem

Fire Gem detonation: remove 3x3 grid around the Fire Gem

Try swapping two Fire Gems together to create a 6x6 detonation. It’s the lowest Special Gem tier, so it’s easier to use the Upgrade Powerup to set up Special Gem swaps. This can get rid of a chunk of Gold Frames on the board quickly.

Rocket Gem: A 2x2 pattern will create a Rocket Gem pointing in a random direction

Rocket Gem detonation


Remove the line in front of the Rocket Gem’s path (shown by a directional arrow on the Rocket Gem) all the way down to the edge of the board

Try swapping two Rocket Gems together to remove the opposing lines of gems on the edge of the board.

Starlight Gems can be made 2 ways; a match of a 5 in a T or L shape.

Starlight Gem: remove a full row and column around a Starlight Gem

Try swapping a Starlight Gem with a Rocket Gem to get tricky gems further away on the same line or edge of the board.

Void Gem: Remove every colored gem that the Void Gem is swapped. Can be swapped with any gem.

Match 5 in a line to make a Void Gem.

Try saving an Upgrade Powerup to create a Special Gem next to a Void Gem! Swap them together to turn the other matching colors into that Special Gem.


Tip: Creating a Void Gem and saving another Special Gem next to it to keep upgrading to make higher tiered Special Gems like a Starlight Gem or another Void Gem can clear higher levels quickly.

A Void Gem x Void Gem Swap will clear the entire board! Use this to clear higher levels with bigger sets of Gold Frames.

Special Gem Swaps
Match Special Gems to detonate them! Swap ANY Special Gems with each other for even bigger impacts! Special Gems can swap together regardless of color/special gem matching to create deadly combo detonations.

Try using certain ones wisely when upgrading gems to the tier that helps destroy tricky corner/edge Gold Frames or to clear the board faster to move to the next level. Sometimes saving detonating gems early can help create a more devastating Special Gem swap on more difficult levels.

Try any of these combinations to see deadly results:

  • Fire + Fire
  • Fire + Rocket
  • Fire + Starlight
  • Rocket + Starlight
  • Rocket + Rocket
  • Starlight + Starlight
  • Void Gem + Void Gem
  • Void Gem + Special Gem

PowerUp Meter
The player fills the PowerUp Meter by earning experience through making matches, chaining falling matches, and detonating Special Gems. Once filled, the player can double tap any gem on the board to upgrade it to the next tier.

  • Tier 1: Fire Gem
  • Tier 2: Rocket Gem
  • Tier 3: Starlight Gem
  • Tier 4: Void Gem

Tip: Use the Upgrade Powerup to make Special Gems adjacent to ones already created on the board. This will set up easy Special Gem Swap combinations.

Leveling & Hyperspeed
After clearing all Gold Frames, you’ll enter Hyperspeed. This doubles all points earned during this duration. Also, one color gem type is removed during this duration, making it easier to get matching color gems next to each other for more matching potential.

Create Special Gems during Hyperspeed to set up powerful chain detonations after leveling up. As you level up, your multiplier will continue to climb. Completing the final level keeps Hyperspeed active till the end of the game for the maximum point multiplier!

Scoring
Make matches as quick as possible to clear gold frames on the board and progress to higher levels. Special Gem swaps will increase chances of continuous chaining that drives up the score. Reaching higher levels will increase the multiplier of all points earned, so clear levels quickly to have the most amount of time in higher multipliers. Clear the final level to reach the highest point multiplier in Infinite Hyperspeed till the end of the game!

Matches Base Score: base scoring of each match made:

  • Making a match of 3: 10 points per gem destroyed in the match
  • Making a match of 4: 20 points per gem destroyed in the match
  • Making a match of 5: 30 points per gem destroyed in the match

PowerUps Base Score: Each Special Gem’s detonation base scoring

  • Fire Gem / Rocket Gem / Starlight Gem: 20 points per gem destroyed in the detonation

Chain Detonations (after matching, falling gems that auto match are chains)

Every level increases this chain scoring:

3 Match - Base Score Chaining

Hyperspeed: 2x point multiplier

  • This multiplier stacks on top of the level multiplier for the duration it's active. Once Hyperspeed ends, the current level multiplier is the only one active.

Tips

  • Use the Powerup Upgrade to create Special Gems faster.
  • Upgrade a Special Gem near Gold Frames and detonate them to remove frames quickly.
  • Swap any two Special Gems with each other and make unique combination explosions. Some combinations have more deadly areas of impact, so swap higher tiered Special Gems for quicker level clear.
  • Double tap any Gem to upgrade to the next tier when the Powerup Meter is active. Upgrade a Gem near a Special one on the board for an immediate Special Swap.
  • Try saving an Upgrade Powerup to create a Special Gem next to a Void Gem! Swap them together to turn the other matching colors into that Special Gem.
  • Swap two Rocket Gems together to destroy two opposite facing columns or rows that have tricky gold frames on the edges of the board.
  • Clear all of the Gold Frames to level up. Get to the final level as fast as possible to maximize the highest point multiplier. Completing all levels keeps Hyperspeed active till time's up.
  • Detonate a Special Gem in the radius of another to create a chain of explosions!

  • Whenever you upgrade a Shape, it is locked for a short duration and is immune to detonation in its radius. Plan ahead and save your Powerup Meter to upgrade a Shape before it's locked in to capitalize on maximum Power Shapes.
